# PermissionScope community conduct

PermissionScope is an engineering project about security-sensitive Windows behavior. Technical disagreement is expected; hostility, intimidation and exclusion are not.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas, evidence and behavior rather than attacking people;
- make uncertainty explicit instead of overstating confidence;
- correct mistakes openly and without humiliation;
- respect privacy and never pressure others to publish sensitive environment data;
- give actionable review feedback with enough context to improve the work;
- welcome contributors with different experience levels, languages and accessibility needs;
- separate strong technical standards from gatekeeping.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking or discriminatory abuse;
- sexualized or degrading remarks;
- deliberate publication of another person's private information;
- doxxing, credential exposure or pressure to share confidential production data;
- repeated bad-faith disruption after a maintainer has explained the project boundary;
- impersonation, fabricated evidence or knowingly false security claims;
- personal attacks disguised as code review.

## Security-sensitive discussions

Do not disclose exploit details for an unpatched vulnerability in a public issue, discussion or pull request. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock or remove content; close issues or pull requests; or restrict participation when conduct materially harms contributors, users or the project's security process.

Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and, when practical, include a short explanation. Severe threats, doxxing, credential exposure or targeted harassment may justify immediate restriction without a prior warning.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments and other spaces represented as official PermissionScope project channels.

## Reporting conduct problems

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through a privacy-preserving channel rather than escalating publicly. If a report also contains a security vulnerability, use the private security-reporting route in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Appeals and conflicts

A participant may ask for a moderation decision to be reconsidered by presenting new evidence or identifying a factual mistake. Repeated argument without new information does not require reopening a decision.

If the project later has multiple maintainers, a maintainer who is personally involved in a conduct dispute should not be the sole reviewer of the appeal when another qualified maintainer is available.
