# PermissionScope free-forever policy

PermissionScope is intended to remain a complete, useful product without purchases, subscriptions, advertising, mandatory accounts or a required cloud connection.

## Project commitment

The official PermissionScope project does not plan to divide the core application into paid and unpaid capability tiers.

The project policy is:

- no paid feature unlocks in the official core application;
- no subscription required for analysis, explanation, snapshots, comparison, simulation or exports;
- no export watermark designed to force payment;
- no artificial usage quota whose purpose is conversion to a paid plan;
- no behavioral advertising inside the product;
- no PermissionScope account required for core local analysis;
- no required cloud backend for core permission decisions;
- no behavioral telemetry monetization.

## Why this exists

PermissionScope deals with security-sensitive evidence. Access to an explanation of a permission result should not depend on whether a user can afford a higher tier.

Keeping the core product free also makes documentation, reproduction and support easier: contributors and users can reason about one capability set instead of multiple commercial editions.

## Open-source relationship

The source is released under the Apache License 2.0. That license grants redistribution and modification rights that are broader than this project's own pricing policy.

As a result, this document governs the **official PermissionScope project and its official distribution decisions**. It cannot force an independent third party to adopt the same pricing or distribution model for a derivative work where the license allows otherwise.

## Sustainability

“Free forever” does not mean that infrastructure, signing certificates, hardware testing or distribution services have no cost. The project may use sponsorship, grants, donations or other non-paywall funding in the future provided those mechanisms do not make payment a requirement for the official core product.

No funding relationship should silently change security conclusions, suppress documented limitations or create undisclosed product access tiers.

## Governance

A proposal that materially weakens this policy is a governance-level change and should be discussed publicly with explicit rationale. See [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).
