# Free forever

PermissionScope is complete without purchases, subscriptions, accounts or a cloud connection. There are no commercial tiers, usage limits, advertising, export watermarks or behavioral telemetry.

All PermissionScope features are available without payment. The source is released under the MIT license. Development tools and third-party components do not impose an end-user subscription.

This is the policy of the PermissionScope project. The MIT license gives others rights to redistribute or modify the software; it does not force independent distributors to follow this project's pricing policy.
